Welcome to the KirigamiAnalysis_ReducedOrderModel repository: a MATLAB code package for performing bar and hinge based simulations of kirigami systems.

The bar and hinge discretization employs bars and hinges for three-dimensional analysis, and bars with torsional springs for in-plane modeling. 
Bars represent in-plane deformation, hinges capture out-of-plane bending, and torsional springs provide rotational stiffness. 
This formulation accommodates a wide range of geometries, boundary conditions, and material properties while maintaining computational efficiency. 
The framework is developed to capture deformation characteristics of kirigami systems, including large deformations, out-of-plane buckling, and in-plane rotational mechanisms.

The repository includes codes for both single-cut kirigami systems and cellular cut-pattern kirigami systems.
